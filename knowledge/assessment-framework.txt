SOP BOT ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORK
Version 1.0.0 | 12 September 2026 | Generic reference, not local policy

Purpose: common terminology for advisory process assessments. Local ownership, permissions, technical availability and approvals are not established by this file.

Review dimensions: purpose; trigger; outcome; scope and exclusions; roles; systems and documents; ordered activities; decisions and approvals; handoffs; escalations; frequency; exceptions; duplication; waiting; errors; rework; and control of versions.

Verdicts:
BUILD: a proportionate candidate for local validation and implementation approval.
PILOT: a promising option needing a bounded test of assumptions or feasibility.
IMPROVE THE PROCESS FIRST: unresolved ambiguity, inconsistency or controls.
ASSIST, BUT KEEP A HUMAN DECISION: support administration or preparation; retain accountable judgement.
NOT WORTH AUTOMATING: effort, maintenance or instability outweigh likely benefit.
DO NOT AUTOMATE: risk, accountability, data quality or exception handling prevents an acceptable option.

Effort bands are planning heuristics inherited from the source guide, not measured performance or Microsoft estimates:
Simple: 2-8 technical hours.
Small: 1-3 technical working days.
Medium: 3-10 technical working days.
Complex: 2-8 weeks or longer.
Converting days or weeks to hours requires an explicit working-hours assumption. Calendar delivery also depends on discovery, permissions, review, testing, training and staff availability.

Capacity calculation:
Gross staff hours per period = minutes saved per case per staff member x cases per period x staff who EACH save that time / 60.
If minutes are already the total across staff, the multiplier is 1.
Realised annual hours = gross hours per active week x active weeks per year x realisation fraction.
Net annual hours = realised annual hours - annual maintenance/support hours.
Average monthly net hours = net annual hours / 12.
Build-only payback months = technical build hours / positive average monthly net hours.
Full implementation payback uses all one-off implementation effort in the numerator.
With zero or negative net monthly hours there is no positive payback. Negative net hours indicate extra workload.

Realisation reflects retained checking, exceptions and adoption. Deductions already included in the minutes should not be applied again. Maintenance is a separate deduction unless explicitly included already. Benefits from overlapping improvements cannot be added twice. Reduced elapsed waiting time is different from reduced staff working time. Capacity released is not automatically a cash saving.

Illustrative calculation, not observed results:
5 gross minutes x 40 cases per active week x 1 staff member / 60 = 3.333 hours per active week.
46 active weeks and 70% realisation give 107.333 realised annual hours.
12 annual maintenance hours give 95.333 net annual hours and 7.944 average net monthly hours.
16 technical build hours / 7.944 = 2.014 months of build-only payback.
Other one-off costs are excluded. Real volumes, checks and support must be measured in a local pilot.

Confidence:
High: specific accessible process evidence and confirmed measurements with limited unresolved assumptions. This is still not implementation approval.
Medium: reasonably clear process with some material assumptions or feasibility questions.
Low: missing, uncertain or conflicting process information or unsupported benefit estimates.

Evidence classes: document fact; user-confirmed context; assumption; estimate; technical possibility. A complete assessment distinguishes these and identifies missing or unreadable source sections.

Assessment structure: overall verdict; what the SOP does; current process; SOP improvements; automation opportunities; what should remain human-led; overall benefit; recommended next step.

Candidate Microsoft tools: Forms, SharePoint, Power Automate, Teams, Outlook, Planner, Power Apps, Power BI, Word templates, Excel/Office Scripts, Microsoft 365 Copilot, Copilot Studio, AI Builder and Power Automate Desktop. These are examples to assess, not a statement that any licence, integration, connector, environment or permission is locally available.
