# Pilot and maintain SOP Bot

First evaluate the **agent** with synthetic material. Then let a small authorised group assess one suitable SOP. Implementing any suggested **workflow improvement** is a separate decision with its own local checks.

## Select the process

Choose a familiar, stable, recurring administrative SOP with a willing process owner, measurable work and material that can be used in your approved environment. Use the [pilot record](../templates/pilot-baseline.md) to capture volume, time, staff contribution, checks, exceptions, systems, rework, waiting and user experience before the assessment.

## Validate the assessment

Ask the owner to label findings **Correct**, **Partly correct**, **Incorrect**, **Missing context**, **Useful**, **Not useful**, or **Requires specialist confirmation**. Check document coverage, role mapping, hidden work, calculations, overlapping savings and retained decisions. Mark factual errors separately from differences in preferred wording.

## Trial one worthwhile change

Choose a low-risk, reversible improvement with confirmed ownership, permissions and a manual fallback. Define scope, duration, success measures and stop conditions before testing. Stop or revert if accountable decisions are bypassed, errors or exceptions cannot be handled, access is inappropriate, or the observed workload outweighs the benefit. The owner determines the next step under local arrangements.

Compare predicted savings with observed staff minutes after retained checking, corrections, exceptions and support. Also measure completion rate, turnaround, confidence, incidents/near misses and adoption. Record what the agent overestimated, underestimated or missed. A recommendation to improve the wording or keep the manual process can be a useful outcome.

## Maintain evidence

Keep the [release log](../templates/release-log.csv), [test evidence](../templates/test-evidence.csv), [source register](../templates/knowledge-source-register.csv) and named owners current. Save local configuration versions and approved source versions together so an earlier setup can be restored.

Re-run affected tests after instruction, knowledge or capability changes. Re-run the full pack before widening access and after material platform/model behaviour changes. Record the date, tenant, response mode, exact input, agent version and reviewer. Do not transfer an old pass result automatically to a changed configuration.

Browser progress is only a convenience on that browser and origin. It is not a shared audit log. Keep substantive evidence and sign-off in your organisation's controlled record system. The guide's export contains checklist states and calculator values, not Copilot responses or independent approval.
