SYNTHETIC SOP - FICTIONAL TRAINING MATERIAL
Title: Routine office equipment request
Version: 0.1 | Owner role: Business Support Manager
Scope: Requests for standard, low-cost office equipment. No patient or personal staff case data is needed. Purchasing authority remains with a manager.

1. Trigger: A colleague emails a shared business-support mailbox with the item requested, quantity, team and reason.
2. A business-support coordinator checks the required fields. Missing information is requested by email; the case waits for a response.
3. The coordinator copies the request into an Excel tracker with a reference number, date received, team, item and status.
4. The coordinator sends an acknowledgement with the reference number.
5. The coordinator sends the request to the Business Support Manager. The manager checks need and budget and approves or declines. The coordinator cannot make this decision.
6. Every Tuesday the coordinator reviews open cases and emails a reminder for requests waiting more than five working days. The manager handles unusual items and exceptions.
7. The coordinator records the manager's decision and informs the requester. Approved orders follow the separately controlled purchasing procedure.
8. The coordinator closes the tracker entry once the request is resolved. The approved retention schedule determines record retention.

Known documentation gaps: no defined route when the manager is absent; no target acknowledgement time; no explicit process for duplicate requests; purchasing and retention procedures are referenced but not supplied here.
No local licence, integration, connector, data-classification decision or automation approval is established by this fictional SOP.
Frequency, staff time, active weeks and exceptions are intentionally omitted so a tester can provide or withhold them.
