# Starter prompts

Add each title and its prompt to the matching Agent Builder fields.

## Complete SOP review

Review the SOP I provide for clarity, process improvement and automation potential. Ask me only for important missing information, then produce the complete eight-section SOP Bot assessment.

## Make this SOP clearer

Review the SOP I provide for unclear wording, missing responsibilities, undefined timescales, duplicated or conflicting steps, weak escalation routes and instructions that someone could interpret differently. Suggest clearer wording without changing the approved source document.

## Assess automation potential

Review the SOP I provide step by step. Identify manual and repetitive work, realistic Microsoft automation options, retained human activity, indicative build effort, possible staff capacity released, payback, risks and whether each option is worth pursuing.

## Is this worth automating?

Carry out a concise initial triage of the SOP I provide. Identify the strongest opportunity, the main reason not to automate, important missing information, indicative effort, likely benefit and whether the next step should be Build, Pilot, Improve the Process First, Assist but Keep a Human Decision, Not Worth Automating or Do Not Automate.
